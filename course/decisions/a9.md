# Assignment 9 And 10 Design Decisions

This file records the key decisions behind Assignments 9 and 10 and the requirements they depend on, and the reason for each.
It is maintainer documentation for the course materials, not part of them: students follow the assignments and the requirements, and this file explains why those read the way they do.
The entries are `A9-DEC-nnn`, zero-padded and never renumbered or reused, in design order, from the two-sprint scope to the order the work is submitted in.
A revision an entry received while the assignment was being written is folded into it, so each entry states the design as it now stands; a later reversal gets a new entry whose `**Reverses:**` names the one it replaces, as a `DEC-nnn` is reversed per [Reversing A Decision](../../requirements/decisions-requirements.md#reversing-a-decision).
An entry that reverses an earlier `An-DEC` records the whole reversal and restates the clauses that survive, because a reversal cannot be partial.
Mechanical edits — backlog housekeeping and the dates a decision here sets — are not design decisions and are not recorded here.

## A9-DEC-001: Two Sprints, Two Submissions, And How The Reviews Are Scheduled

- **Status:** Accepted
- **Decision:** `SPRINT-09` is Week 9 and Submission 9 is due `2026-11-29`; `SPRINT-10` is Week 10 and Submission 10 is due `2026-12-03`; `SPRINT-10`'s milestone and provisional section are created in Week 9 and it is planned in Week 10; each submission has its own Moodle wrapper and assignment page.
  The Sprint 9 review is held in person, at a time arranged with the customer, by `2026-11-29`, and a review on the deadline day is allowed.
  The Sprint 10 review and handoff are one in-person meeting at a time arranged with the customer, and this plan sets no latest day for it.
  The final exam is `2026-12-04` in the syllabus's Week 11 section, and the individual reflection and peer evaluation is Submission 11, due `2026-12-05`, in that section.
  Sprint 10 closes after `REL-10`.
  The assignment catalog separates the ten group submissions from the final exam, Submission 11, and attendance, so no item number competes with a submission number.
- **Why:** the course grades Week 10 as well as Week 9, and one course week is one sprint with its own submission; a single final submission cannot carry the Sprint 9 review, the UAT run, and the Week 10 handoff.
  Both reviews are customer meetings held in person, so the date and the hour are arranged with the customer rather than set by a deadline-day rule; the submission deadlines still bound the week.
- **Alternatives considered:** one final submission with Week 9 folded into Week 10, rejected because the Sprint 9 review and its UAT run would lose their record; a Week 10 with no submission, rejected because the final state, the deck, and the final report would have no deadline; a fixed review day, rejected because an in-person meeting is arranged with the customer.
- **Where it lands:** [the plan](../plans/a9-plan.md#design-decisions), [the syllabus](../syllabus.md), `assignments/assignment-9.md`, and `assignments/assignment-10.md`.

## A9-DEC-004: REL-10 Is Cut After The Handoff And The UAT Re-run Verifies The Candidate

- **Status:** Accepted
- **Decision:** `REL-09` is cut at the Sprint 9 close; `REL-10` is cut after the Sprint 10 handoff, on the corrected final state, and before `2026-12-03` 23:59; the handoff presents the final candidate, the customer agrees any corrections, and `REL-10` is cut on the corrected state; the Week 10 report records the tag and any delta; `REL-10` may be a docs-only release.

- **Alternatives considered:** cutting `REL-10` before the handoff, rejected because the customer could not review the final state; cutting it after the deadline, rejected because no public change is allowed after 23:59; a third customer run on `REL-10`, rejected because the plan has two runs and a failed run is recorded instead.

## A9-DEC-005: The Assessment Is Ten Group Submissions, 107 Points, And 75 To Pass

- **Status:** Accepted
- **Decision:** the course has ten group submissions at 7 points each (70), 10 points of attendance, 7 points of individual reflection and peer evaluation (Submission 11), and 20 points of final exam, for 107 points; the pass threshold is 75 points, not 75% of 107; the late penalty is 10% of the submission's points per calendar day, more than 7 days late receives zero, and a 7-point submission loses 0.7 points per day; repository upkeep is assessed inside the ten submissions; Submission 9 is due `2026-11-29`; Submission 11 is due `2026-12-05` and is available only to a member of a group that presented at the final exam.
  The assignment catalog carries the ten group submissions in the assignment table, and the final exam, Submission 11, and attendance in a separate non-assignment section with no submission numbers.
  The weight shift from the old percentages is deliberate: the group submissions carry 70 of the 107 points, attendance 10, the individual reflection 7, and the final exam 20.
- **Why:** the course grades Week 10, so it has a tenth group submission; points keep the arithmetic visible and the late penalty proportional to the submission; the 75-point pass is the maintainer's chosen threshold; the catalog split keeps the submission numbers and the assessment-item numbers from competing.
- **Alternatives considered:** keeping percentages, rejected because 107 points does not divide cleanly into 100%; reading the pass as 75% of 107 (80.25 points), rejected because the maintainer set 75 points; a separate repository-upkeep component, rejected because upkeep is assessed inside the submissions; keeping item numbers on the final exam and Submission 11, rejected because they are not assignments.
- **Where it lands:** [the syllabus](../syllabus.md) and [the plan](../plans/a9-plan.md#design-decisions).

## A9-DEC-006: The Final Sprint Has No Later Sprint

- **Status:** Accepted
- **Decision:** at the Sprint 9 close, an open issue moves to `SPRINT-10`; at the Sprint 10 close, the completed issues stay in the closed milestone, and every open issue, including the ones scheduled and not completed, leaves the milestone, stays open, clears its `Responsible`, `Accountable`, and assignees, and is listed in the Week 10 report's `## Handoff`; the final retrospective says there is no later sprint, a `Not done` action is `Outcome: Not done` in `## Carried Actions`, and the `## Actions` carry `Carried: No`; the project reflection is independent of the retrospective's actions.
- **Why:** there is no later sprint to receive an open issue or a carried action; an open issue in a closed milestone would claim work the sprint did not finish; the retrospective's carried-action table already has the `Outcome` value.
- **Alternatives considered:** leaving open issues in the closed milestone, rejected because the milestone would be a false claim; carrying the retrospective actions into the reflection, rejected because the reflection is the project's lessons; re-listing a not-done action in `## Actions`, rejected because the carried-action table already owns it.
- **Where it lands:** [Planning Requirements](../../requirements/planning-requirements.md#sprints), [Issue Tracking Requirements](../../requirements/issue-tracking-requirements.md#scheduling-an-issue), and [Sprint Ceremonies Requirements](../../requirements/sprint-ceremonies-requirements.md#retrospective).

## A9-DEC-017: The UAT-nn Family And The Customer's Two Runs

- **Status:** Accepted
- **Decision:** the user acceptance tests are the `UAT-nn` family, two digits, maintained at `docs/user-acceptance-tests.md`, one end-to-end journey each; each section states the journey on the first line, then `**Status:**`, then the start state, the steps, the expected result, and a runs table with one row per run: sprint, date, `Customer` as the runner, result, evidence, and `DEC-nnn`; the evidence is the sanitized meeting report's anchor and the run's decision, and a run is cited as the test's section plus the sprint, such as `docs/user-acceptance-tests.md#uat-01` and `SPRINT-09`; the record has a `## The Tests` index.
  The team defines exactly three tests, `UAT-01` to `UAT-03`; the customer runs them at the Sprint 9 review, one `DEC-nnn` for the set, and re-runs them on the release candidate at the Sprint 10 handoff, a separate `DEC-nnn`; the handoff acceptance is its own `DEC-nnn`; there is no post-handoff re-run and a failed run is recorded as `Fail`; both runs are in the record; the Week 9 and Week 10 reports link their run; the final report cites the results; the UATs replace the core-task verification on `REL-09` and `REL-10`, and the Weeks 5–8 deploys keep it; the `UT-01` results are linked only.
- **Why:** the customer is the acceptance authority, not the team; the runs table keeps each run's evidence and decision; the release candidate is what the customer can run before `REL-10`; a post-handoff re-run would be a third run the plan does not need, and the failure is recorded instead; the evidence cell needs a public, sanitized anchor, and the meeting report is it.
- **Alternatives considered:** a per-test `DEC-nnn` instead of a row per run, rejected because each run has its own verdict; a post-handoff re-run, rejected because the failure is recorded and the claim is narrower; a team-run UAT, rejected because the customer is the authority; a per-run anchor, rejected because the record's anchor is the test section and the sprint names the run.
- **Where it lands:** `requirements/user-acceptance-tests-requirements.md`, [the plan](../plans/a9-plan.md#design-decisions), and [Customer Meetings Requirements](../../requirements/customer-meetings-requirements.md).

## A9-DEC-018: The Checked UATs Field Is Added And A7-DEC-021 Is Reversed

- **Status:** Accepted
- **Reverses:** [`A7-DEC-021`](a7.md#a7-dec-021-the-test-is-not-a-uat)'s clause that no `**Checked UATs:**` field is added to a release record; the surviving clauses are that the usability test is not a UAT and that the Weeks 5–8 production deploys keep the core-task verification.
- **Decision:** the `REL-09` and `REL-10` blocks carry `**Checked UATs:**` beside `**Checked QRs:**`, the field's scope is those two blocks only, and the line links the `docs/user-acceptance-tests.md` run and its sprint.
  `repository-requirements.md`'s rule 7 is replaced for those two deploys by the customer-run three `UAT-nn` on the release candidate, and the Weeks 5–8 deploys keep the core-task rule.
  The `TODO.md` UAT production-verification item is closed, because the UAT work it was waiting for is designed.
  The status of `A7-DEC-021` becomes `Reversed by A9-DEC-018`.
- **Why:** `A7-DEC-021` deferred the field because no UAT was designed; the UATs now exist and verify `REL-09` and `REL-10`, so the field has a real value to carry and the deferral is over; the two releases are the only ones whose production verification is the customer's run.
- **Alternatives considered:** leaving the field out and recording the UAT verification only in the reports, rejected because the release record would then miss the check that replaced the core task; editing `A7-DEC-021` in place, rejected because a written entry is not rewritten; adding the field to every release from `REL-05`, rejected because the Weeks 5–8 releases keep the core-task rule and would carry `None`.
- **Where it lands:** [Planning Requirements](../../requirements/planning-requirements.md#releases), [Releases Requirements](../../requirements/releases-requirements.md#cutting-a-release), [Repository Requirements](../../requirements/repository-requirements.md#environments-and-deployment), and [A7-DEC-021](a7.md#a7-dec-021-the-test-is-not-a-uat).
