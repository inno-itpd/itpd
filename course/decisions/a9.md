# Assignment 9 And 10 Design Decisions

This file records the key decisions behind Assignments 9 and 10 and the requirements they depend on, and the reason for each.
It is maintainer documentation for the course materials, not part of them: students follow the assignments and the requirements, and this file explains why those read the way they do.
The entries are `A9-DEC-nnn`, zero-padded and never renumbered or reused, in design order, from the two-sprint scope to the order the work is submitted in.
A revision an entry received while the assignment was being written is folded into it, so each entry states the design as it now stands; a later reversal gets a new entry whose `**Reverses:**` names the one it replaces, as a `DEC-nnn` is reversed per [Reversing A Decision](../../requirements/decisions-requirements.md#reversing-a-decision).
An entry that reverses an earlier `An-DEC` records the whole reversal and restates the clauses that survive, because a reversal cannot be partial.
Mechanical edits — backlog housekeeping and the dates a decision here sets — are not design decisions and are not recorded here.

## A9-DEC-001: Two Sprints, Two Submissions, And How The Reviews Are Scheduled

- **Status:** Accepted
- **Decision:** `SPRINT-09` is Week 9 and Submission 9 is due `2026-11-29`; Week 9's class dates stay 20–26 November, and the Sprint 9 review may be held by the sprint's due date of `2026-11-29`, including the days after the class week.
  `SPRINT-10` starts `2026-11-30` and is due `2026-12-06`; Submission 10 is due `2026-12-06`.
  Submission 10 has a hard deadline of Sunday 6 December 2026 at 23:59.
  Moodle accepts no late submission for Assignment 10.
  Create `SPRINT-10`'s milestone and provisional section in Week 9 and plan it after the Sprint 9 review.
  Plan 60 minutes for the in-person Sprint 10 review and handoff by `2026-12-06`.
  The customer attends the handoff.
  The extra days after the Week 10 class window give teams time to hold the handoff and finish the final report.
  The final exam is `2026-12-04` in the syllabus's Week 11 section.

- **Where it lands:** [the plan](../plans/a9-plan.md#design-decisions), [the syllabus](../syllabus.md), `assignments/assignment-9.md`, and `assignments/assignment-10.md`.

## A9-DEC-005: The Assessment Is Ten Group Submissions, 107 Points, And 75 To Pass

- **Status:** Accepted
- **Decision:** the course has ten group submissions at 7 points each (70), 10 points of attendance, 7 points of individual reflection and peer evaluation (Submission 11), and 20 points of final exam, for 107 points; the pass threshold is 75 points of the 107; the late penalty is 10% of the submission's points per calendar day, more than 7 days late receives zero, and a 7-point submission loses 0.7 points per day; repository upkeep is assessed inside the ten submissions; Submission 9 is due `2026-11-29`; Submission 11 is due `2026-12-06` and is available only to a member of a group that presented at the final exam.
  The late penalty applies to submissions that accept late work.
  Assignment 10 has a hard cutoff and accepts no late submission.
  The assignment catalog carries the ten group submissions in the assignment table, and the final exam, Submission 11, and attendance in a separate non-assignment section.
  The weight shift from the old percentages is deliberate: the group submissions carry 70 of the 107 points, attendance 10, the individual reflection 7, and the final exam 20.
- **Why:** the course grades Week 10, so it has a tenth group submission; points keep the arithmetic visible and the late penalty proportional to the submission; 75 points of the 107 is the maintainer's chosen threshold; the catalog split keeps the submission numbers and the assessment-item numbers from competing.
- **Alternatives considered:** keeping percentages, rejected because 107 points does not divide cleanly into 100%; reading the pass as 75% of 107 (80.25 points), rejected because the maintainer set 75 points; a separate repository-upkeep component, rejected because upkeep is assessed inside the submissions; keeping item numbers on the final exam and Submission 11, rejected because they are not assignments.
- **Where it lands:** [the syllabus](../syllabus.md#late-submission-policy) and [the plan](../plans/a9-plan.md#design-decisions).

## A9-DEC-006: The Final Sprint Has No Later Sprint

- **Status:** Accepted
- **Decision:** at the Sprint 9 close, only issues necessary to make the UATs pass and selected documentation work may move to `SPRINT-10`; other open issues remain unscheduled, outside sprint and plan milestones; at the Sprint 10 close, the completed issues stay in the closed milestone, and every open issue, including the ones scheduled and not completed, leaves the milestone, stays open, clears its `Responsible`, `Accountable`, and assignees, and is listed in `handoff.md`; the final retrospective says there is no later sprint, a `Not done` action is `Outcome: Not done` in `## Carried Actions`, and the `## Actions` carry `Carried: No`; the project reflection is independent of the retrospective's actions.
  The final-sprint exception overrides retrospective rule 4: a `Not done` action is not re-listed in `## Actions`, because there is no next retrospective.
- **Why:** there is no later sprint to receive an open issue or a carried action; an open issue in a closed milestone would claim work the sprint did not finish; the retrospective's carried-action table already has the `Outcome` value.
- **Alternatives considered:** leaving open issues in the closed milestone, rejected because the milestone would be a false claim; carrying the retrospective actions into the reflection, rejected because the reflection is the project's lessons; re-listing a not-done action in `## Actions`, rejected because the carried-action table already owns it and there is no next sprint.
- **Where it lands:** [Planning Requirements](../../requirements/planning-requirements.md#sprints), [Issue Tracking Requirements](../../requirements/issue-tracking-requirements.md#scheduling-an-issue), and [Sprint Ceremonies Requirements](../../requirements/sprint-ceremonies-requirements.md#retrospective).
