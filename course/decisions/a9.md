# Assignment 9 And 10 Design Decisions

This file records the key decisions behind Assignments 9 and 10 and the requirements they depend on, and the reason for each.
It is maintainer documentation for the course materials, not part of them: students follow the assignments and the requirements, and this file explains why those read the way they do.
The entries are `A9-DEC-nnn`, zero-padded and never renumbered or reused, in design order, from the two-sprint scope to the order the work is submitted in.
A revision an entry received while the assignment was being written is folded into it, so each entry states the design as it now stands; a later reversal gets a new entry whose `**Reverses:**` names the one it replaces, as a `DEC-nnn` is reversed per [Reversing A Decision](../../requirements/decisions-requirements.md#reversing-a-decision).
An entry that reverses an earlier `An-DEC` records the whole reversal and restates the clauses that survive, because a reversal cannot be partial.
Mechanical edits — backlog housekeeping and the dates a decision here sets — are not design decisions and are not recorded here.

## A9-DEC-001: Two Sprints, Two Submissions, And The Final-Exam Page

- **Status:** Accepted
- **Decision:** `SPRINT-09` is Week 9 and Submission 9 is due `2026-11-28`; `SPRINT-10` is Week 10 and Submission 10 is due `2026-12-03`; `SPRINT-10`'s milestone and provisional section are created in Week 9 and it is planned in Week 10; each submission has its own Moodle wrapper and assignment page; the final exam is `2026-12-04` in `assignments/final.md`; the individual reflection and peer evaluation is Submission 11, due `2026-12-05`, in `assignments/final.md`; both reviews may be same-day, with Thursday 26 November and Wednesday 2 December recommended, and Sprint 10 closes after `REL-10`.
- **Why:** the course grades Week 10 as well as Week 9, and one course week is one sprint with its own submission; a single final submission cannot carry the Sprint 9 review, the UAT run, and the Week 10 handoff.
- **Alternatives considered:** one final submission with Week 9 folded into Week 10, rejected because the Sprint 9 review and its UAT run would lose their record; a Week 10 with no submission, rejected because the final state, the deck, and the final report would have no deadline.
- **Where it lands:** [the plan](../plans/a9-plan.md#design-decisions), [the syllabus](../syllabus.md), `assignments/assignment-9.md`, `assignments/assignment-10.md`, and `assignments/final.md`.

## A9-DEC-002: The Final Report Is The Week 10 Report And The Reflection Is Separate

- **Status:** Accepted
- **Decision:** `reports/week-09/README.md` is the Week 9 report and owns the UAT results and `## Handoff preparation`; `reports/week-10/README.md` is the Week 10 report and the final report and owns `## Handoff`; `reports/week-10/reflection.md` is the project reflection; there is no `reports/week-10/final-report.md`; Submission 9 carries no final-report draft; no file in `reports/week-09/` is rewritten after Submission 9.
- **Why:** the final report needs the final state, the handoff, and the UAT re-run, which exist only after Week 10; the Week 9 report is the Sprint 9 record; the reflection's lessons are a different artifact from the final report's narrative.
- **Alternatives considered:** keeping the final report in `reports/week-09/`, rejected because it cannot contain the Week 10 state; a separate `final-report.md`, rejected because the weekly report rules and the index role already own the final report; a final-report draft in Submission 9, rejected because it would be written before the handoff.
- **Where it lands:** [Weekly Report Requirements](../../requirements/weekly-report-requirements.md#weekly-public-report) and [the plan](../plans/a9-plan.md#design-decisions).

## A9-DEC-003: The Handoff Is The Sprint 10 Review And The Week 10 Report's Handoff

- **Status:** Accepted
- **Decision:** the handoff is part of the Sprint 10 review, one meeting with one script written before it and one report written after it; the Week 9 report owns `## Handoff preparation`; the Week 10 report owns `## Handoff` and cites `reports/week-10/meeting-report.md`; public rows link public evidence and private rows point at the wrapper's `## For customer`; the customer's verdict is a `DEC-nnn` — acceptance, conditional acceptance, or refusal; there is no post-handoff re-run and a failed test is recorded; the `REL-10` tag is recorded in the Week 10 report, not the meeting report.
- **Why:** the handoff is the review's outcome rather than a second meeting; the Week 10 report is the durable record; the customer's verdict and a failure both need a written decision.
- **Alternatives considered:** a separate handoff meeting, rejected because the review is not a second meeting; the meeting report carrying the tag, rejected because `REL-10` is cut after the meeting; a post-handoff re-run, rejected because the failed run is recorded and the claim is narrower.
- **Where it lands:** [Customer Meetings Requirements](../../requirements/customer-meetings-requirements.md), [Weekly Report Requirements](../../requirements/weekly-report-requirements.md#weekly-public-report), and [the plan](../plans/a9-plan.md#design-decisions).

## A9-DEC-004: REL-10 Is Cut After The Handoff

- **Status:** Accepted
- **Decision:** `REL-09` is cut at the Sprint 9 close; `REL-10` is cut after the Sprint 10 handoff, on the corrected final state, and before `2026-12-03` 23:59; the handoff presents the final candidate, the customer agrees any corrections, and `REL-10` is cut on the corrected state; the Week 10 report records the tag and any delta; `REL-10` may be a docs-only release.
- **Why:** the handoff can change the product, so the final release must be the state the customer accepted; the deadline still binds.
- **Alternatives considered:** cutting `REL-10` before the handoff, rejected because the customer could not review the final state; cutting it after the deadline, rejected because no public change is allowed after 23:59.
- **Where it lands:** [Planning Requirements](../../requirements/planning-requirements.md#releases), [Releases Requirements](../../requirements/releases-requirements.md#cutting-a-release), and [the plan](../plans/a9-plan.md#design-decisions).

## A9-DEC-005: The Assessment Is Ten Group Submissions, 107 Points, And 75 To Pass

- **Status:** Accepted
- **Decision:** the course has ten group submissions at 7 points each (70), 10 points of attendance, 7 points of individual reflection and peer evaluation (Submission 11), and 20 points of final exam, for 107 points; the pass threshold is 75 points, not 75% of 107; the late penalty is 10% of the submission's points per calendar day, more than 7 days late receives zero, and a 7-point submission loses 0.7 points per day; repository upkeep is assessed inside the ten submissions; Submission 11 is due `2026-12-05` and is available only to a member of a group that presented at the final exam.
- **Why:** the course grades Week 10, so it has a tenth group submission; points keep the arithmetic visible and the late penalty proportional to the submission; the 75-point pass is the maintainer's chosen threshold.
- **Alternatives considered:** keeping percentages, rejected because 107 points does not divide cleanly into 100%; reading the pass as 75% of 107 (80.25 points), rejected because the maintainer set 75 points; a separate repository-upkeep component, rejected because upkeep is assessed inside the submissions.
- **Where it lands:** [the syllabus](../syllabus.md) and [the plan](../plans/a9-plan.md#design-decisions).

## A9-DEC-006: The Final Sprint Has No Later Sprint

- **Status:** Accepted
- **Decision:** at the Sprint 9 close, an open issue moves to `SPRINT-10`; at the Sprint 10 close, the completed issues stay in the closed milestone, and every open issue, including the ones scheduled and not completed, leaves the milestone, stays open, clears its `Responsible`, `Accountable`, and assignees, and is listed in the Week 10 report's `## Handoff`; the final retrospective says there is no later sprint, a `Not done` action is `Outcome: Not done` in `## Carried Actions`, and the `## Actions` carry `Carried: No`; the project reflection is independent of the retrospective's actions.
- **Why:** there is no later sprint to receive an open issue or a carried action; an open issue in a closed milestone would claim work the sprint did not finish; the retrospective's carried-action table already has the `Outcome` value.
- **Alternatives considered:** leaving open issues in the closed milestone, rejected because the milestone would be a false claim; carrying the retrospective actions into the reflection, rejected because the reflection is the project's lessons; re-listing a not-done action in `## Actions`, rejected because the carried-action table already owns it.
- **Where it lands:** [Planning Requirements](../../requirements/planning-requirements.md#sprints), [Issue Tracking Requirements](../../requirements/issue-tracking-requirements.md#scheduling-an-issue), and [Sprint Ceremonies Requirements](../../requirements/sprint-ceremonies-requirements.md#retrospective).

## A9-DEC-008: The Final Report Keeps The Weekly Report Rules And Groups The Final Outcomes By Status

- **Status:** Accepted
- **Decision:** the Week 10 report is the weekly report and the final report; it keeps rules 1 to 23, with rule 11 recording the final product at `REL-10` and rule 16 not required in Weeks 9 and 10; `## Threshold review` keeps rule 17's table for the `Open` goals and adds a final-outcomes table grouped by status — `TOS-nn` by `Achieved`, `Missed`, `Open`, and `Dropped`, and `QR-nn QRT-nn` by `Passes`, `Fails`, `Not verified`, and `Dropped`, with identifier order inside each group; it copies no full testing-plan row; the final report does not link `docs/analytics.md` or `docs/observability.md` and does not say `Not available`; the focus metric is cited only if it has a value; `docs/threshold-of-success.md` and the testing plan keep their order and their statuses and outcomes are updated first.

## A9-DEC-010: SPRINT-10 Is Created In Week 9 And Planned In Week 10

- **Status:** Accepted
- **Decision:** `SPRINT-10`'s GitHub milestone and its `### SPRINT-10` section with a provisional goal are created in Week 9; it is planned in Week 10 like every other sprint; its goal is a result; its `**Sprint Backlog:**` links its milestone; its `**Planned Velocity:**` counts the stories it carries, and a task-only sprint has `0` and says so; it refines only its own backlog, because there are no next two sprints; it sits outside every `MLS-nn` run, and no `MLS-10` is written.

## A9-DEC-016: The Private Data Is Deleted After Grading And The Main Repository Is Privatised Only With Approval

- **Status:** Accepted
- **Decision:** the private usability-data repository and the recordings stay private until the course is fully graded, and the team then deletes them; the main repository is public during the course; after the course is fully graded, the team may make it private only with the instructors' approval; the MIT licence, the Moodle snapshot, and the instructors' access before grading remain; the handoff and the wrapper record the retention and the mandatory deletion; there is no transfer of private data.
- **Why:** deletion is mandatory so the platform cannot leak the data; the main repository is a course-wide public artifact; privatisation after grading needs the instructors' consent because it changes who can open the snapshot.
- **Alternatives considered:** optional deletion, rejected because the platform risk is the reason to delete; transferring the private data, rejected because the visibility rule keeps it with the team and the instructors; keeping the main repository private during the course, rejected because the course's public model is a course-wide decision.
- **Where it lands:** [Visibility Requirements](../../requirements/visibility-requirements.md#the-usability-data-repository), [Repository Requirements](../../requirements/repository-requirements.md#repository-setup), and [Weekly Report Requirements](../../requirements/weekly-report-requirements.md#private-submission-wrapper).

## A9-DEC-017: The UAT-nn Family And The Customer's Two Runs

- **Status:** Accepted
- **Decision:** the user acceptance tests are the `UAT-nn` family, two digits, maintained at `docs/user-acceptance-tests.md`, one end-to-end journey each; each carries its journey, start state, steps, expected result, and a runs table with one row per run: sprint, date, `Customer`, result, evidence, and `DEC-nnn`; the record has a `## The Tests` index; the team defines `UAT-01` to `UAT-03`; the customer runs them at the Sprint 9 review, one `DEC-nnn` for the set, and re-runs them on the release candidate at the Sprint 10 handoff, a separate `DEC-nnn`; the handoff acceptance is its own `DEC-nnn`; there is no post-handoff re-run and a failed run is recorded as `Fail`; both runs are in the record; the Week 9 and Week 10 reports link their run; the final report cites the results; the UATs replace the core-task verification on `REL-09` and `REL-10`, and the Weeks 5–8 deploys keep it; the `UT-01` results are linked only.
- **Why:** the customer is the acceptance authority, not the team; the runs table keeps each run's evidence and decision; the release candidate is what the customer can run before `REL-10`; a post-handoff re-run would be a third run the plan does not need, and the failure is recorded instead.
- **Alternatives considered:** a per-test `DEC-nnn` instead of a row per run, rejected because each run has its own verdict; a post-handoff re-run, rejected because the failure is recorded and the claim is narrower; a team-run UAT, rejected because the customer is the authority.
- **Where it lands:** `requirements/user-acceptance-tests-requirements.md`, [the plan](../plans/a9-plan.md#design-decisions), and [Customer Meetings Requirements](../../requirements/customer-meetings-requirements.md).
