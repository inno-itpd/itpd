# Plan: Assignments 9 And 10 — The Final Two Sprints, The Two Submissions, The UATs, The Handoff, And The Reflection

This plan designs Assignment 9 and Assignment 10 as [`a8-plan.md`](a8-plan.md) designs Assignment 8: two sprints, `SPRINT-09` in Week 9 and `SPRINT-10` in Week 10, two graded group submissions, two releases `REL-09` and `REL-10`, the three user acceptance tests, the handoff, the final report, the project reflection, the individual reflection and peer evaluation, the final repository state, the two weekly reports, the two retrospectives, the two AI usage reports, and the two Moodle wrappers.
It states the scope and the contradictions the wider materials expose, with the resolution each takes, in [Scope And Contradictions](#scope-and-contradictions).
The final exam is a separate course event described in `assignments/final.md`; this plan does not design it, and no assignment part depends on it.

Week 9 is `SPRINT-09` and Submission 9, due **Saturday 28 November 2026 at 23:59**.
Week 10 is `SPRINT-10` and Submission 10, due **Thursday 3 December 2026 at 23:59**.
The Sprint 9 review is the Week 9 weekly customer meeting, held by Saturday 28 November; Thursday 26 November is recommended, and a same-day review on the deadline is allowed.
The Sprint 10 review is the Week 10 weekly customer meeting and the handoff, held by Thursday 3 December; Wednesday 2 December is recommended, and a same-day review on the deadline is allowed.
A sprint closes after its review and its release: `SPRINT-09` closes after `REL-09`, and `SPRINT-10` closes after `REL-10`, at the latest on `2026-12-03` 23:59.
`REL-09` is cut at the Sprint 9 close; `REL-10` is cut after the Sprint 10 handoff, on the corrected final state, and before `2026-12-03` 23:59.
The product code freezes at `REL-10`, and the public repository and the product take no change after `2026-12-03` 23:59.
The main repository is public during the course; after the course has been fully graded, the team may make it private only with the instructors' approval.
The private usability-data repository and the recordings folder stay private until the course has been fully graded, and the team deletes them after grading; that mandatory deletion and the approved post-grading privatisation are the only later changes.

The course has ten graded group submissions: Submission 1 to Submission 10, each worth 7 points, so 70 of the 107 points.
The other components are 10 points of class attendance, 7 points of individual reflection and peer evaluation, and 20 points of final exam, for 107 points in total.
The pass threshold is 75 points of the 107, not 75% of 107.
The late submission policy is charged in points: 10% of the submission's points per calendar day after the hard deadline, so a 7-point submission has a maximum of 6.3 points one day late, and a submission more than 7 days late receives zero.
The individual reflection and peer evaluation stays, is submitted after the final exam, and is renumbered Submission 11.
Repository upkeep is assessed inside the ten submissions; it is not a separate component.
Submission 9 and Submission 10 are separate.

The `UT-01` results are not re-presented in the final two weeks; the Week 9 report, the Week 10 report, and the final report link the Week 8 report only where the evidence needs it.

## Design Decisions

Week 9 and Week 10 are two sprints, two submissions, two reviews, two releases, one final handoff, one final report, one project reflection, one individual reflection, one final state, one `docs/user-acceptance-tests.md`, and three customer-run `UAT-nn`.

- **The two sprints and the two submissions.**
  `SPRINT-09` runs in Week 9 and is due `2026-11-28`; `SPRINT-10` runs in Week 10 and is due `2026-12-03`.
  `SPRINT-10`'s milestone and its `### SPRINT-10` section with a provisional goal are created in Week 9; it is planned in Week 10.
  Submission 9 is due `2026-11-28`; Submission 10 is due `2026-12-03`; each is a Moodle submission and each has its own assignment page.
  The individual reflection and peer evaluation is Submission 11, due `2026-12-05` 23:59, available only to a member of a group that presented at the final exam; it stays individual, private, and Moodle-only.
  The course has ten group submissions at 7 points (70), 10 points attendance, 7 points individual reflection and peer evaluation, and 20 points final exam, for 107 points; the pass threshold is 75 points; the late penalty is 10% of the submission's points per calendar day.
  The final exam is a separate event in `assignments/final.md`.
- **Sprint 9.**
  `SPRINT-09` is planned like every other sprint: its provisional goal is replaced by a planned goal, its `**Sprint Backlog:**` links its GitHub milestone, its `**Planned Velocity:**` counts the user stories it schedules, and its `**Actual Velocity:**` is set at the close.
  The UAT tasks, the handoff preparation, the Week 9 report, the retrospective, and the AI usage report are tasks with no story points; no deck task is scheduled; a product story carried from `SPRINT-08` or added in Week 9 counts.
  The sprint's review is the Week 9 weekly customer meeting, held by Saturday 28 November, with Thursday 26 November recommended and a same-day review allowed; it shows the Sprint 9 increment, the customer runs the three `UAT-nn`, and its script asks what the customer expects to receive as the handoff.
  `REL-09` is cut at the close, after the review and any same-week rework; the Week 9 report, the handoff preparation, the UAT results, and the retrospective are submitted by `2026-11-28` 23:59.
  Every open issue at the close moves to `SPRINT-10`, because the later sprint exists.
- **Sprint 10.**
  `SPRINT-10` is the final sprint; it is planned in Week 10 like every other sprint, and it may carry user stories or only tasks.
  Its goal is a result, not a list of artifacts; its `**Sprint Backlog:**` links its GitHub milestone; its `**Planned Velocity:**` counts the user stories it schedules, and a task-only sprint has `0` story points and says so.
  The final sprint refines only its own backlog, because there are no next two sprints to refine for.
  It sits outside every `MLS-nn` run, which the planning rule allows.
  Its review is the Week 10 weekly customer meeting and the handoff; the review and the handoff are one meeting, held by Thursday 3 December, with Wednesday 2 December recommended and a same-day review allowed; the review shows the final candidate, the final deck, the Week 10 report draft, and the handoff package, and the customer re-runs the three `UAT-nn` on the release candidate.
  The sprint closes after the review, the customer's corrections, and `REL-10`; it does not close at the handoff.
  At the close, the issues completed in the sprint stay in the closed `SPRINT-10` milestone; every open issue, including the ones scheduled in the sprint and not completed, stays open with no milestone, clears its `Responsible`, `Accountable`, and assignees, and is listed with its link and why it is open in the Week 10 report's `## Handoff`; there is no later sprint.
- **The reviews and the handoff.**
  There are two Sprint Reviews, one per sprint: the Week 9 customer meeting reviews Sprint 9, and the Week 10 customer meeting reviews Sprint 10 and hands the product over.
  The Week 9 review's script is written before the meeting and asks what the customer expects to receive as the handoff, alongside the questions the Sprint 9 increment needs; the review shows the increment, the customer runs the three `UAT-nn`, and the review records the customer's verdict on the Sprint 9 stories as `DEC-nnn` entries and the UAT set as one `DEC-nnn`.
  The Week 9 report's `## Handoff preparation` is written after the review: it records the customer's expectations, what is ready, and what is missing, and it reports the UAT results.
  The Week 10 review shows the final candidate, the final deck, the Week 10 report draft, and the handoff package, the customer re-runs the three `UAT-nn` on the release candidate, and the review records the final story verdicts, the UAT re-run as a separate `DEC-nnn`, and the handoff acceptance as a `DEC-nnn`.
  The Week 10 review and the handoff are one meeting with one script written before it and one meeting report written after it, `reports/week-10/meeting-report.md`; there is no second meeting and no `meeting-report-2.md`.
  The Week 10 report's `## Handoff` is the handoff report: one row per item, its status, and where it lives, with public rows linking public evidence and private rows saying the value is in the Moodle wrapper's `## For customer`, plus the link to the Week 9 preparation and the citation of the meeting report for the customer's verdict (acceptance, conditional acceptance, or refusal) and the UAT re-run.
  The handoff is live; the asynchronous rule is a last resort with the written exchange as the transcript and a declared deviation.
  The handoff records the final candidate and the planned `REL-10` release, the repository, the deployed MVP, the UAT re-run results, the documentation, the dashboards and access, the credentials, the known issues, the open items, the mandatory private-data deletion, and the customer's access; the private values are in the wrapper.
- **The user acceptance tests.**
  The team defines `UAT-01` to `UAT-03` in `docs/user-acceptance-tests.md` before the Sprint 9 review; each covers one end-to-end user journey and carries its journey, start state, steps, expected result, run sprint, result, evidence, and `DEC-nnn`.
  The customer runs the three tests on the deployed product at the Sprint 9 review; the team records each pass or fail; the customer's verdict on the set is one `DEC-nnn`.
  The customer re-runs the three tests on the release candidate at the Sprint 10 handoff; the re-run is a separate `DEC-nnn` on top of the handoff acceptance; there is no post-handoff re-run.
  If the Sprint 9 review's rework changes a journey, the affected `UAT-nn` is re-run by the customer before `REL-09`, or the Week 9 report declares the delta, and the Sprint 10 re-run covers the final state.
  Both runs are recorded in `docs/user-acceptance-tests.md`; the Week 9 report links its run, the Week 10 report links its re-run, and the final report cites the results.
  The three `UAT-nn` replace the core-task verification on both the `REL-09` and `REL-10` production deploys; the Weeks 5–8 deploys keep the core-task rule.
  The `UT-01` results are not re-presented in the final two weeks; the reports link the Week 8 report only where the evidence needs it.
  The UATs are a new requirements file and a new identifier family; they are not a `QRT-nn`, and the UAT record is `docs/user-acceptance-tests.md`.
- **The releases, the MVP, and the code freeze.**
  `REL-09` is the Week 9 release, cut at the Sprint 9 close on the final Sprint 9 product commit, after the UAT run and the review's rework.
  `REL-10` is the final release and the MVP, cut after the handoff on the corrected final state and before `2026-12-03` 23:59.
  The handoff presents the final candidate; the handoff report records that `REL-10` is cut after the meeting, and the Week 10 report records the final tag and commit.
  `REL-10` may be a product release or a docs-only release; either way its tag is the handoff version, and a docs-only release says so and requires no upgrade.

  The plan's `#### REL-09` and `#### REL-10` blocks carry `**Released:**`, `**Checked QRs:**`, and `**Checked UATs:**`; a sprint with no checked requirement says `None` and keeps the testing-plan sentence; the `**Checked UATs:**` line links the `docs/user-acceptance-tests.md` run.
  Product code freezes at `REL-10`; docs, the reports, the reflection, the changelog, and the release blocks may merge between `REL-10` and `2026-12-03` 23:59; no public change of any kind is made after `2026-12-03` 23:59.

## Edge Cases And Failure Modes

- **The Sprint 9 review slips past the deadline.**
  The review is held by `2026-11-28` 23:59, with Thursday 26 November recommended and a same-day review allowed; a review after the submission is a missing deliverable for Submission 9 and a deviation for Submission 10, and the Week 10 report declares the slip; the Week 9 report is not rewritten.
- **The Sprint 10 handoff slips past the deadline.**
  The handoff is held by `2026-12-03` 23:59, with Wednesday 2 December recommended and a same-day review allowed; a handoff after the deadline is a missing deliverable; the Week 10 report cannot declare a slip after its own submission, so the wrapper or `assignments/final.md` carries it, and the submission still goes in.
- **The handoff finds something that needs product code.**
  The team agrees the correction at the handoff, applies it before `REL-10`, and cuts `REL-10` on the corrected state before `2026-12-03` 23:59; if it cannot land, it becomes an open item in the Week 10 report's `## Handoff`, a risk, and a task for the next project, and the final report says so; the customer's verdict (acceptance, conditional acceptance, or refusal) may be conditional, and the `DEC-nnn` records the condition.
- **The customer rejects the handoff.**
  The Sprint 10 meeting report records the rejection and the open items, the customer's decision is a `DEC-nnn`, and the Week 10 report's `## Handoff` says what was not accepted; no product code changes after `REL-10`, and the submission still goes in.
- **`REL-10` is not ready before the handoff.**
  The handoff presents the final candidate and the customer agrees the release is cut after the meeting; the Week 10 report records the tag and any delta; `REL-10` is still cut after the handoff and before `2026-12-03` 23:59.
- **A correction lands after `REL-10`.**
  It does not: product code is frozen at `REL-10`; a correction that cannot land is an open item, not a second final release; a hotfix after `REL-10` is not made, and if one is made it is a declared deviation and the final state is not the handoff version.
- **A UAT fails at the Sprint 9 review.**
  The team fixes it before `REL-09` if the fix fits the same week, or it becomes a known issue or an open item; the customer's `DEC-nnn` for the set records the failure, the Week 9 report reports it, and the final report does not claim that journey passes.
- **A UAT fails at the Sprint 10 re-run.**
  The Sprint 10 meeting report records the failure, the separate re-run `DEC-nnn` records it, the handoff acceptance is conditional or refused, the Week 10 report's `## Handoff` says so, and the final report says the MVP claim is narrower; there is no post-handoff re-run, and a correction that lands before `REL-10` is not re-verified by the customer.
- **The Sprint 9 review's rework changes a UAT journey.**
  The affected `UAT-nn` is re-run by the customer before `REL-09`, or the Week 9 report declares the delta and the Sprint 10 re-run covers the final state; a rework that silently changes a journey leaves the `REL-09` verification stale.
- **The customer cannot run a UAT.**
  The tests are customer-run, so the team does not substitute for the customer; the run is rescheduled inside the review window, or the missing run is a missing deliverable and a declared deviation, and the final report claims no pass for that journey.
- **A UAT journey changes between the two runs.**
  The updated test is recorded in `docs/user-acceptance-tests.md`, the two runs are kept with their dates, and the Week 10 report declares the difference.
- **The `REL-09` or `REL-10` deploy is verified by the core task.**
  It is not: the three `UAT-nn` replace the core-task verification on those two deploys, and the Weeks 5–8 deploys keep the core-task rule; a core-task check on `REL-09` or `REL-10` is a declared deviation.
- **The final report or the project reflection is not ready for Submission 10.**
  The Week 10 report and the project reflection are required deliverables; a missing one is a missing deliverable, not a deviation; the team writes the Week 10 report from the evidence and the reflection from the project, and the deadline is not moved.
- **The pitch deck is not ready for Submission 10.**
  The one deck is a required deliverable; a missing deck is a missing deliverable, not a deviation, and there is no draft from Submission 9 to fall back on; the team finalizes it before the submission, and its content is the later session.
- **The final exam uses a newer deck than the submitted one.**
  The final exam is a separate event in `assignments/final.md`; this plan submits the deck by `2026-12-03` 23:59 and does not design it; a change to the deck for the exam is outside this plan.
- **An issue cannot close before the final state.**
  The issues completed in `SPRINT-10` stay in its closed milestone; an issue scheduled in `SPRINT-10` and not completed leaves the milestone, stays open with no milestone, clears its `Responsible`, `Accountable`, and assignees, and is listed with its link and why it is open in the Week 10 report's `## Handoff`; there is no later sprint.
- **The final retrospective has no next retrospective.**
  It says there is no later sprint; a `Not done` action is `Outcome: Not done` in `## Carried Actions`, and the Sprint 10 retrospective's `## Actions` carry `Carried: No`, and nothing is carried into the project reflection; the reflection is the project's lessons, not the retrospective's next home.
